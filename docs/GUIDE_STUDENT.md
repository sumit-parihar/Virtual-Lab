# Student guide

Sign in with the email and temporary password you received. The first time, you choose a new password. If you forget it, use
**Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes.

## The menu
**Home** (general Machine Learning content), **Experiments**, **View Progress**, and your profile icon (**My Profile**, **Log out**).

## Doing an experiment
Open **Experiments** and choose one. The list on the left shows the sections in order. An empty circle means not done yet; a
filled circle means done.

| Section | How it is completed |
|---|---|
| Title / Overview, Aim, Video, Theory, Guided Lab, Code Exercise | Click **Mark Completed** at the end of the section. |
| Pre-Quiz, Post-Quiz | Submit it once. You can try as many times as you like; your best score is kept. |
| Assignment | Submit it once. It cannot be repeated. |

- **Quizzes:** click **Start Now**, answer, and submit. You see this attempt's score and your best score.
- **Code Exercise:** write your answer in the editor, store the final value in the variable `result`, and click **Run** to see it.
  Click **Submit** to check it: you see your output and **Correct** or **Incorrect**. If the exercise has a dataset file, load it by
  the file name shown above the editor.
- **Assignment:** it opens after every earlier section is complete. If you click it too early, a message lists what is missing.
  After you submit, you see your score and each question with the correct answer and yours.

## View Progress
Shows your overall percentage and each experiment as **Not Attempted**, **In Progress** or **Completed**. Click an experiment to see
its sections. **View Assignment Scores** lists your assignment scores.

## My Profile
Shows your name, email, URN and batch. **Change password** is here. Ask the administrator if something is wrong.

# Instructor guide

Sign in with the email and temporary password you received. You are asked to choose a new password the first time. If you
forget it, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

## Your batches
**Batches** shows the batches assigned to you. Open one to see its details and the students (URN and name). You can search,
sort and page through the list. Students the administrator has deactivated stay in the list marked **Inactive**.

If no batch is shown, the administrator has not assigned one to you yet.

## Experiments
In a batch, open **Experiments** and choose one. You see the same material as the students (overview, aim, quizzes, video,
theory) with the correct answers shown. You cannot attempt anything.

Click **Students Status** to see, for each student, **Not Attempted**, **In Progress** or **Completed**. **Download Excel** saves
this table for the experiment you have open.

## Assignments
In a batch, open **Assignment** and choose one. You see the questions with the correct answers.

Click **Assignment Status** for each student's marks (a percentage) or **Not Submitted**. **View Submission** opens that student's
answers, marked correct or incorrect. It is disabled until the student has submitted. **Download Excel** saves the scores.

## What you do not see
Students' Pre-Quiz and Post-Quiz scores are private and are not shown. You cannot add or change students, batches or experiments:
ask the administrator.
